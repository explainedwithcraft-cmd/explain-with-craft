Privacy Policy

Explain with Craft upload tool · Last updated: 10 November 2026

What this app is

The Explain with Craft upload tool is a private application used only by the owner of the Explain with Craft channel to publish the channel's own videos to its own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

With the owner's permission, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read the basic details of the owner's own channel (its name), so it can confirm where videos are published. It does not access any other user's data.

How data is stored and shared

  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared.
  • No personal data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising is used by the tool.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
